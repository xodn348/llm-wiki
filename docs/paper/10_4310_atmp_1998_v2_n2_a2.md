# Anti de Sitter space and holography

## Why this mattered

Witten’s paper turned Maldacena’s AdS/CFT conjecture from a striking equivalence into a usable dictionary. Its central step was to identify conformal field theory correlation functions with the response of the bulk supergravity action to specified boundary values of fields at infinity. This made holography operational: boundary sources coupled to CFT operators, bulk fields encoded those operators, and operator dimensions were related to particle masses in AdS. The proposal thereby converted a broad duality claim into a calculational framework for comparing quantum field theory observables with classical gravity or string theory.

The importance was not only that strongly coupled large-\(N\) gauge theories could be studied using weakly coupled gravity, but that gravity itself could be reformulated as a lower-dimensional quantum theory without gravity. This sharpened the holographic principle into a concrete example in which spacetime geometry, black holes, thermodynamics, and quantum field theory data were tied together by explicit equations. The discussion of the finite-temperature transition in \(\mathcal{N}=4\) super Yang-Mills theory and its relation to AdS black holes also showed that phases of gauge theory could be mapped onto gravitational phenomena.

After this paper, AdS/CFT became a working tool rather than mainly a conjectural analogy. It underpinned later developments in holographic renormalization, black hole entropy and information, strongly coupled plasma physics, entanglement entropy, quantum gravity, and mathematical studies of conformal field theory. Together with Maldacena’s original proposal and the related Gubser-Klebanov-Polyakov formulation, Witten’s prescription established the standard GKPW dictionary, which remains one of the central technical foundations of holography.

## Abstract

Recently, it has been proposed by Maldacena that large N limits of certain conformal field theories in d dimensions can be described in terms of supergravity (and string theory) on the product of d 4-1dimensional AdS space with a compact manifold. Here we elaborate on this idea and propose a precise correspondence between conformal field theory observables and those of supergravity: correlation functions in conformal field theory are given by the dependence of the supergravity action on the asymptotic behavior at infinity. In particular, dimensions of operators in conformal field theory are given by masses of particles in supergravity. As quantitative confirmation of this correspondence, we note that the Kaluza-Klein modes of Type IIB supergravity on AdS^ x S 5 match with the chiral operators of Af = 4 super Yang-Mills theory in four dimensions. With some further assumptions, one can deduce a H'amiltonian version of the correspondence and show that the Af = 4 theory has a large N phase transition related to the thermodynamics of AdS black holes.
