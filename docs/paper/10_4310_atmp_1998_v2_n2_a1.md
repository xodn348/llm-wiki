# The large $N$ limit of superconformal field theories and supergravity

## Why this mattered

Maldacena’s paper transformed a set of suggestive parallels between branes, black holes, gauge theories, and string theory into a concrete duality: certain large-$N$ conformal field theories are equivalent to string or M-theory on Anti-de Sitter backgrounds. The central shift was not merely that gravity could emerge from a nongravitational quantum field theory, but that a theory with no dynamical spacetime metric could encode a higher-dimensional gravitational spacetime. This gave the holographic principle its first precise and calculable realization, especially through the proposed duality between type IIB string theory on $AdS_5 \times S^5$ and four-dimensional $\mathcal{N}=4$ super-Yang-Mills theory.

The paper made previously inaccessible questions newly tractable in both directions. Strongly coupled gauge dynamics could be studied using weakly curved classical gravity when $N$ and the ’t Hooft coupling are large; conversely, aspects of quantum gravity could be defined through an ordinary quantum field theory. This turned black hole thermodynamics, entropy, absorption, correlation functions, confinement-like phenomena, and renormalization-group flow into problems with a shared dictionary rather than separate formalisms. The result was a practical bridge between string theory and quantum field theory, not just a philosophical claim about holography.

Its influence shaped much of high-energy theory after 1998. The AdS/CFT correspondence became the leading nonperturbative definition of string theory in asymptotically AdS settings, a testing ground for quantum gravity, and a toolkit for strongly coupled systems. Subsequent breakthroughs, including the Gubser-Klebanov-Polyakov/Witten prescription for correlators, holographic renormalization, gauge/gravity models of quark-gluon plasma, entanglement entropy via Ryu-Takayanagi surfaces, and modern work on black holes, quantum information, and emergent spacetime, all trace directly to the possibility opened by this paper: gravitational geometry can be reconstructed from, and exactly dual to, quantum many-body dynamics.

## Abstract

We show that the large N limit of certain conformal field theories in various dimensions include in their Hilbert space a sector describing supergravity on the product of Anti-deSitter spacetimes, spheres and other compact manifolds.This is shown by taking some branes in the full M/string theory and then taking a low energy limit where the field theory on the brane decouples from the bulk.We observe that, in this limit, we can still trust the near horizon geometry for large N.The enhanced supersymmetries of the near horizon geometry correspond to the extra supersymmetry generators present in the superconformal group (as opposed to just the super-Poincare group).The 't Hooft limit of 3+1 J\f = 4 super-Yang-Mills at the conformal point is shown to contain strings: they are IIB strings.We conjecture that compactifications of M/string theory on various Anti-deSitter spacetimes is dual to various conformal field theories.This leads to a new proposal for a definition of M-theory which could be extended to include five noncompact dimensions.
