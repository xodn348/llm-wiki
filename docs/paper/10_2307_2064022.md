# Frame Analysis: An Essay on the Organization of Experience.

## Why this mattered

*Frame Analysis* mattered because it shifted attention from experience as a direct response to objective situations toward experience as something organized through socially learned interpretive structures. Goffman’s central question, “What is it that’s going on here?”, made it possible to analyze reality-testing, role performance, play, deception, ritual, media events, and everyday interaction under a common conceptual vocabulary: frames, keys, fabrications, and transformations. The paradigm shift was not simply that people “interpret” events, but that interpretation itself has patterned rules that can be studied sociologically.

After this work, scholars could treat meaning-making as an empirical object rather than a residual category left outside institutional or behavioral explanation. That opened a path for later breakthroughs in social movement theory, media studies, political communication, cognitive sociology, and discourse analysis, where “framing” became a way to explain how actors define problems, assign blame, mobilize publics, and make some courses of action appear natural while others seem unthinkable. Gamson’s later work on collective action frames and media discourse is one major continuation of this line.

Its lasting importance is that it connected micro-sociology to large-scale cultural and political change. Frames operate in face-to-face encounters, but they also shape journalism, protest, legal reasoning, scientific controversy, and public memory. The result was a durable bridge between symbolic interactionism and later theories of cognition, culture, and power: social reality could be studied not only as something imposed by institutions, but as something continuously organized, stabilized, contested, and revised through shared frames.

## Abstract

Erving Goffman will influence the thinking and perceptions of generations to come. In Frame Analysis, the brilliant theorist writes about the ways in which people determine their answers to the questions What is going on here? and Under what circumstances do we think things are real?
