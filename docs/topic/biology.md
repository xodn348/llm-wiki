# Biology

_128 Fleming-tier papers._

## Synthesis

These Fleming-tier biology papers collectively trace the conversion of biology from a largely organismal and descriptive science into an experimental, molecular, computational, and translational discipline. One dominant lineage runs from Darwinian evolution and sexual selection through formal population genetics, social evolution, neutral theory tests, F-statistics, molecular phylogenetics, DNA barcoding, and Bayesian tree inference. A second lineage builds the laboratory infrastructure of modern molecular biology: tissue culture media, model-organism staging for chick, *C. elegans*, zebrafish, and *Arabidopsis*, recombinant plasmid screening, Southern blotting, nick translation, PCR, transformation, cloning vectors, RNA polymerase extracts, monoclonal antibodies, and targeted gene disruption. Together these works made heredity, development, gene regulation, and evolutionary history experimentally tractable at nucleotide resolution.

The major paradigm shifts are the molecularization of biology, the rise of model organisms, the genomic and post-genomic turn, and the reframing of disease as molecular classification and cellular mechanism. Cancer papers move from altered metabolism and oncogenes to HER2, BRAF, expression-defined tumor classes, and tumor-initiating cells. Stem-cell papers move from human embryonic stem cells and mesenchymal potential to induced pluripotency and matrix-directed fate. RNA biology is transformed by lin-4 small RNAs, RNA interference, SELEX, and exosome-mediated RNA transfer. The later titles connect biology tightly to adjacent fields: statistics and computer science through maximum likelihood, bootstrapping, PCA, Bioconductor, KEGG, Gene Ontology, PDB, and gene-set analysis; medicine through cancer genomics and biomarkers; ecology and nutrition through microbiome studies; and engineering through standardized databases, assays, and transformation systems.
